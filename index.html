What is Strokes Gained? ▼

Strokes Gained (SG) measures how many strokes you gain or lose compared to a scratch golfer benchmark on each shot. A positive number means you performed better than scratch; negative means worse.

SG: Off the Tee
Tee shots on par 4s and par 5s. Based on whether you hit the fairway (FIR).
SG: Approach
Shots into the green. Calculated from your approach distance — longer shots are harder.
SG: Around the Green
Chipping, pitching and bunker play when you miss the green (no GIR).
SG: Putting
All putts once on the green. Based on number of putts vs scratch average.

How to read your numbers: If your SG: Putting is +0.8 over 10 rounds, you gain nearly a stroke per round on the greens vs scratch. If SG: Approach is −1.2, that's your biggest area to improve. Focus practice where your SG is most negative.

Benchmarks are based on PGA Tour scratch-golfer averages (Broadie model). Figures are approximate for amateur use.
